# RemindIt — app description

## Short version

RemindIt is a Progressive Web App that replaces paper scraps and notes apps with a clean shopping list that lives on your phone, tablet, and browser. Tap items from your catalog to build your list, let smart recommendations learn your shopping rhythm and flag what's overdue or due soon, and check things off as you go — then share the rest as a polished image with whoever's shopping with you. Everything is saved locally, works fully offline, and an account is optional: sign in only if you want to sync across devices or shop together on a shared list.

## Long version

RemindIt is a privacy-first Progressive Web App for managing your shopping list. It installs on any device like a native app, works offline, and keeps your data on your device — an account is optional, and nothing is uploaded until you sign in.

**Build your list**

- Tap items from your catalog to add or remove them; your list stays grouped by category and you can check items off as you shop.
- **Quick add**: a grouped autocomplete suggests items by category and surfaces your recommendations as you type; category pills create a new item instantly.
- **Smart recommendations**: colored pips flag items that are overdue or due soon based on how often you buy them, and get smarter the more you shop.
- **History**: review your last 7 days of adds and removes, grouped by day.

**Keep your catalog in shape**

- Manage items and categories in collapsible sections sorted by how often you reach for them, alphabetical (A–Z) within each category, with a distinct color per category.
- Rename by double-click (desktop) or tap (mobile); swipe to delete on mobile, all confirmed in dialogs.

**Share & personalize**

- **Share**: turn the items you still need into a polished card image — copy it to your clipboard or download it as a PNG and send it to whoever's shopping with you.
- **Personalize**: pick a categorical color palette (Van Gogh is the default), a dark/light/system theme, and English or Romanian — your choices are remembered.
- **Install as an app**: add RemindIt to your home screen and use it offline like a native app, with an in-app changelog tracking what's new.

**Privacy & sync**

- **Local-first**: everything is saved in your browser; nothing is uploaded until you sign in.
- **Sync (optional)**: keep your list, catalog, and history in step across all your devices — still fully offline-capable.
- **Shared lists**: shop together — the owner invites people by exact username, everyone sees the same items in real time, and members can switch or leave anytime.
- **Backup & restore**: download your data as a JSON backup and restore it on any device, even during first-run setup.

**What's coming**

- Notifications about activity on your shared lists.
- Item attributes (photo, quantity, price).
